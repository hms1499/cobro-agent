Questions, answered

Is Cobro a bank?+

No. Cobro is software. Your money sits in stablecoins on Celo, in a wallet set up for you, and you can move it to your own wallet at any time.

What does it cost?+

Creating invoices is free. Conversions happen at market rates through Textile, and the small network fee is paid from your balance in dollars, never in a token you have to buy.

Which currencies can my clients use?+

USA₮, USD₮, wARS and wBRL on Celo, from wallets like MetaMask, Rabby or MiniPay.

What if the agent makes a bad trade?+

It can't trade outside your limits or below a fair price, and it never sells dollars without asking unless you allow it. You can pause it with one click.

How do I cash out?+

Withdraw to your own Cobro wallet, then send to the exchange or app you already use to turn stablecoins into cash.
